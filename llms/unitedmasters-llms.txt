# UnitedMasters

> UnitedMasters is an independent music distribution platform founded by Steve Stoute in 2017, backed by Alphabet, Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), and Apple. Artists distribute music to 150+ streaming services while keeping full ownership of their master recordings, with real-time royalty payouts, Blueprint AI artist-development tools, analytics, and brand/sync licensing partnerships (NBA, ESPN, TikTok, and others). UnitedMasters does not currently publish a public developer API, SDKs, or API documentation.
